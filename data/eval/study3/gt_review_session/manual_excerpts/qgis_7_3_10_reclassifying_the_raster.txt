7.3.10 ★★☆ Follow Along: Reclassifying the Raster
We have used the Raster calculator to do calculations on raster layers. There is another powerful tool that we can use
to extract information from existing layers.
Back to the aspect layer. We know now that it has numerical values within a range from 0 through 360. What we
want to do is to reclassify this layer to other discrete values (from 1 to 4), depending on the aspect:
• 1 = North (from 0 to 45 and from 315 to 360);
• 2 = East (from 45 to 135)
• 3 = South (from 135 to 225)
• 4 = West (from 225 to 315)
