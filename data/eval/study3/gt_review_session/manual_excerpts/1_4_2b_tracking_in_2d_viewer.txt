Note: A 3D horizon as well as Fault stick sets can also be interpreted in the 2D viewer.

Workflow cont'd:

3D horizon interpretation using a 2D viewer

1. The horizon type you are interpreting is 3D horizon.

2. The tracking modes are similar but on the contrary to the 2D tracking, the auto-tracking
option is ON while tracking a 3D horizon in a 2D viewer.

3. For auto-tracking the horizon, recommendation is to use the Seed trace method in
combination with the section auto-track option (see previous exercise 1.4.2a for more
details).

4. In the 2D viewer, while tracking a 3D horizon, you can switch inline/crossline by either
typing the line number or using the arrows and the step to interpret a grid like you would
do in the 3D scene.
